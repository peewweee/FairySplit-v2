Geometry

  • Zero radius everywhere. Cards, rows, buttons, the sheet, calendar cells — all hard corners. Softness comes entirely from the palette and the spacing.
  • Outline borders 1.8px near-black. Cancel and "See everyone's share" get a heavy dark stroke, not a timid grey one.
  • Card borders 1px hairline with almost no shadow — the reference separates surfaces with line, not elevation.
  • Only true circles keep a radius — the help and back buttons, the status dot, the decorative arcs — plus the device bezel and home indicator, which are hardware, not interface.
  • Arc bleed — thin clipped circles at card corners. The signature detail.

Type

  • Figtree, 400–900. The original is almost certainly Gilroy, which is commercial; Figtree is the closest free match — geometric, double-storey a, single-storey g, tall x-height.
  • One family, no exceptions — the wordmark is Figtree 800 too, the way paié sits in its own type rather than a separate logo face.
  • Tracking −.035em on headings and values against relaxed 500-weight captions. Most of the perceived polish lives in that contrast.
  • Tabular numerals everywhere money appears.

On contrast. The reference sets its grey captions very light, which fails WCAG AA at small sizes. I darkened the warm grey to #7E6A70 — 5.0:1 on white, still reading as a quiet caption. Every text pair here clears AA; the pink is only ever a background with near-black on top, never text on white.

On the pink. #EF9BB8 carries the same job amber did: light enough for dark text at 8.6:1, saturated enough to be the one loud thing per screen. #B5416B is its readable sibling for small accents where the soft pink would disappear.
